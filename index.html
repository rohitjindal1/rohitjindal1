A bit about my work

I'm a senior mobile application developer with 7.7 years of experience building scalable, high-performance apps in Flutter (Dart), native Android (Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose), and real-time systems on Agora.   • Categorised income & expense tracking with dedicated icons

  • Clean, consistent UI built with attention to spacing and hierarchy
